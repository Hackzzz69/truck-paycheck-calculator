How this is calculated
W-2: truck revenue = Σ of your load totals; gross = 15% of truck revenue; per diem = 7.95% (exact on all 6 stubs); federal = IRS Pub. 15-T 2026 weekly tables (pre-2020 W-4) on gross, rounded to whole dollars, minus $2,000/yr per dependent; Oklahoma ≈ 4.48% of gross − $9.16, rounded to dollars (fitted — exact within 35¢); Social Security/Medicare = (rounded YTD tax at new YTD) − (rounded YTD tax at prior YTD). 1099: check = 25% of revenue; set aside 15.3% × 92.35% of profit for SE tax plus a weekly slice of projected federal income tax (2026 MFJ/single/HoH brackets & standard deductions, child credits, EIC). Head of household: weekly withholding uses the Single table (the pre-2020 W-4 has no HoH checkbox — HoH filers check Single); the annual projection uses HoH brackets and the $24,150 standard deduction. State tax not modeled.
